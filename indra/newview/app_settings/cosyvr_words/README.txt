CosyVR Viewer on-screen keyboard word lists
==================================

One word a line, most frequent first, for each keyboard layout's language (file name: the
language code; pt is Brazilian Portuguese). The keyboard suggests and swipes these words
under what it learns from the chat logs. Azerbaijani has no list yet.

Source: Hermit Dave, FrequencyWords, 2018 lists (content/2018/<lang>/<lang>_50k.txt),
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus
(http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).

License: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as the source
lists. Changed for CosyVR Viewer: kept only words of 2 to 24 letters, lower case, letters only; the
counts dropped; cut to the first 30,000 words. These lists are shared under the same license.
